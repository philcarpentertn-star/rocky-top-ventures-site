# Street Hoops: One-minute challenge

Press Start to begin a 60-second round. Take as many shots as the 1.4-second shot animation allows. Four shooting positions work by tapping a player, using the four buttons, or pressing 1–4 with the court focused. The hoop moves continuously through shots; aim for where it will be when the ball reaches the rim. Movement stays continuous between shots and level changes.

Five levels advance every 12 seconds. The clock runs through shots and level notifications and continues in background tabs. Shots released strictly before 60 seconds finish and count after the buzzer. Starting a new round resets the timer and requires Start again. Zero-shot rounds finish normally.

Two points per make, three points from the third consecutive make onward. Server replay validates absolute shot timestamps, cooldowns, player selection, the deadline, and minimum round duration. Basketball scores and personal bests use v6; football remains v3.

Movement reference: [original cabinet gameplay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLIF61EK8M4&t=97s) shows the backboard moving during ball flight.

Artwork is a new recreation inspired by Hoop Jones. Prompts and provenance are in `assets/ARTWORK.md`.
